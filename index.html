How it works

  1. Download the starter folder and read the project description.
  2. Build the project on the real board and fill in the folders as shown below.
  3. Zip it, choose your team number, enter your team code and upload the zip here.
  4. Within a minute or two this page shows your automated check result. You can fix problems and upload again: the latest upload before the deadline is the one that counts.

Your team code was sent to your team by the instructor. Keep it private: it is the only thing that lets someone submit for your team.

How your zip must be organised

TEAM.md                 team members, contributions, demo video link
firmware/
  main.c
  mcal/                 register-level drivers (.c / .h)
  hal/                  hal.h (do not change the signatures) and hal.c
  app/                  app.c, app.h: protocol, alarm, door events
mobile-app/             your mobile app project (source files only)
report/
  report.pdf            the technical report, one PDF file
  • Zip the contents of the folder. A single top-level folder inside the zip is fine too.
  • Mobile app: export your App Inventor project (Projects → Export selected project (.aia)) and put the .aia file in mobile-app/. The check reads its blocks. Blocks that are disabled or not inside any event or procedure never run, so they do not count.
  • TEAM.md: 4 to 6 members, each with a contribution, and a line Demo video link: https://…. Do not put the video in the zip: upload it to YouTube or Google Drive and paste the link.
  • firmware/app/ may only include hal.h and standard C headers: no TM4C registers or headers, no MCAL includes. The HAL may include the MCAL.
  • Source files only: no .axf, .o, build or IDE folders. Maximum zip size is 20 MB.
What the automated check does (and does not) cover

It checks the layout above, runs your firmware/app/*.c on a PC against a simulated board (lamp, plug, door, temperature every second, alarm, STATUS?, message handling), checks that each layer has real code and respects the layer rules, and reads your App Inventor blocks for the protocol, the clock polling, the alert and the door log. Mounting and the report are marked by the instructors from your video and report, and they also check the app on a phone, so a perfect automated result is not the final mark.
